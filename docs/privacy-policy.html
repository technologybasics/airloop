Airloop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Airloop ("the app") is a breathing exercise app. This policy explains what data the app collects — which, as of this version, is none.

Data collection

Airloop does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. The app has no backend, no user accounts, and no analytics or tracking SDKs of any kind.

Data stored on your device

Your session history, streak, and settings (such as cycle counts and haptics preferences) are stored locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage. This data never leaves your device, is never shared with us or any third party, and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

Third parties

Airloop does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-sharing services.

Changes to this policy

If Airloop's data practices change in a future update (for example, if optional cloud sync is added), this policy will be updated first, and the change will be reflected in the app's App Store privacy disclosures.

Contact
