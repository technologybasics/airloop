## Description

Airloop is a simple, focused breathing app for calming down, winding down, or resetting your focus — in under five minutes.

Four guided techniques, each with a clear rhythm and a gentle animated circle to follow:
- Box breathing (4-4-4-4) — calm and focus
- 4-7-8 breathing — wind down and fall asleep faster
- Coherent breathing (5-5) — balance and steadiness
- Calm breathing (6-6) — slow, steady relaxation

Airloop tracks your practice streak and shows your history on a simple calendar, so you can see your consistency build over time.

Built for daily use, not distraction:
- No account or sign-in required
- No ads, no analytics, no data collection — your session history stays only on your device
- Subtle haptic feedback at each phase change, so you can practice eyes-closed
- Clean, minimal design that gets out of your way

Whether you need to calm down before a meeting, wind down before bed, or just take five minutes to breathe, Airloop is built to be the fastest way back to steady.
